# NorthStar Navigator

A plain-language government benefits navigator for Minnesota. Helps residents understand which government assistance programs they may be eligible for based on their situation.

**Languages:** English, Spanish, Hmong, Somali

**Model:** Fine-tuned Gemma 4 E4B (q4_k_m GGUF) via QLoRA on ~10K multilingual training examples.

Built for the Kaggle "Gemma 4 Good Hackathon."
